## Context

Team project titled "Requirements gathering / Discovering the economic and
ecological environment". The goal was to create a corporate website presenting
Capgemini, a digital services company, to middle school students (aged 14–15),
while analyzing its digital and ecological transition.

## Goals

- Research and summarize information about the company: business, products and
  services, organization, competitors, clients, funding, strategy and values.
- Analyze its digital and ecological transition, its constraints and its
  benefits.
- Design a mockup, then build a simple, eco-designed website.
- Produce a company fact sheet.

## Implementation

### Phase 1: requirements gathering

In-depth research on Capgemini from a variety of sources, to get a complete and
objective view. From it we produced a concise fact sheet, using management and
economics vocabulary explained in a way the target audience could follow. The
analysis then focused on the company's initiatives in sustainable development
and eco-design.

### Phase 2: design and development

We proposed a site architecture based on the information gathered, then made
mockups in Whimsical according to the client's needs. Feedback from the
mid-term review helped us refine the mockups. The site was then built in HTML5
and CSS3, with a clean design following eco-design principles, using Visual
Studio Code and GitHub for collaboration.

## Results

The site meets its goal: presenting the company in an educational way to
middle school students, while highlighting its environmental commitments. Above
all, this project made me practice turning a client's need into a site
architecture, and explaining things to a non-technical audience.
